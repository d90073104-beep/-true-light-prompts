# Natural Image Enhancement Prompts

Two distinct prompts for enhancing photos naturally while preserving the original scene without adding non-existent elements. Choose the prompt that matches your photo type.

---

## Prompt 1: Low-Light & Night Enhancement (Night / Dark Photos Only)
Use this prompt for dark, night, or low-light photos.

Brighten dark areas cleanly, lift heavy shadows, and balance highlights and contrast while preserving the natural night lighting mood and rich colors. Reduce low-light noise smoothly without over-sharpening or inventing artificial light sources. Keep the exact original scene and display the enhanced image directly as a normal saveable image.

---

## Prompt 2: Universal Clarity & Color Enhancement (General / All Lighting Photos)
Use this prompt for general photos in daylight, indoor, or mixed lighting without forcing a night look.

Enhance this image with high clarity, crisp detail, and rich natural color vibrancy across any lighting condition. Sharpen edges and surface textures smoothly without over-sharpening, harshness, or grain. Keep the exact original scene, objects, and lighting faithful to the source image without forcing a night look or adding non-existent elements like sun or sunset. Display the enhanced image directly as a normal saveable image.

---

## How to Choose
* **Prompt 1 (Night / Dark):** Best when the photo is dark or shot at night and needs shadow recovery and noise reduction.
* **Prompt 2 (General / All):** Best for everyday photos across lighting conditions when you want natural clarity and colors without a night effect.
